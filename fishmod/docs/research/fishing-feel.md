# Study: what makes acclaimed fishing feel good

Part of the fishmod research (see [README.md](README.md)). It builds on [fishing-games.md](fishing-games.md), which covers TP's bobber
bite, journal and species, and Sega Bass Fishing's follow-and-flick and tension gauge; this doesn't repeat those.

**How it was researched:** the research sandbox couldn't open the pages themselves, so every claim comes from
search-result summaries of the cited pages. Treat specifics as "reported by", not verified.

## The short version

The fishing systems people praise (OoT's pond, TP, Sega Bass Fishing, FFXV, Red Dead Redemption 2, Sea of Thieves,
Bass Masters Classic) agree on a handful of things, and the ones people criticise (Stardew's bar, Dredge's rings,
Fishing Resort's pose prompts, Legend of the River King 2's mashing) break them:

1. **The fish is the UI.** Intent is read off the fish and the rod (a follow, a turn toward the lure, a bulge on
   the surface, rumble, rod bend), not off a meter. A gauge, if any, only confirms what the body shows.
2. **The real decisions come before the bite:** where to cast, which bait or lure, and how to work it.
3. **The bite has a rhythm and three cues:** nibble, nibble, BITE, shown by the bobber, a sound and the rumble. The
   hook-set window opens the instant the bite happens.
4. **The fight is driven by the fish, not by chance.** It runs in a direction you can see; you steer against it,
   reel while it's tired and ease off when it surges. The line breaks because of something you did.
5. **Fight phases are telegraphed:** run, tire, head shake, jump. A jump is a moment to give slack, not a prompt.
6. **Failure teaches:** say why it got away ("too early", "you reeled while it ran", "it threw the hook on the jump").
7. **Progress is knowledge:** time of day, weather, spot, bait. Rare fish are rare because of conditions, not dice.
8. **No dead time:** players praise "you won't wait long" (Bass Masters, Sega Bass Fishing).

## What each game does

**Ocarina of Time (our own baseline).** B casts (hold for distance) and tugs the lure; the stick wiggles it; A
reels, A+R reels fast; A with the stick pulled down sets the hook; in the fight the stick steers as the fish turns
(held sideways too long, the fish gets away). It rumbles with the fish. Knowledge: dawn, dusk and rain bite better;
big fish hold by the log and reeds; the loach lives deep and surfaces now and then; the sinking lure is "prohibited".
Reviewers liked the finesse but call it more frustrating than TP: one pond, hard to see the fish.
([StrategyWiki](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Ocarina_of_Time/Minigames),
[Wikibooks](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Ocarina_of_Time/Minigames),
[GameWith](https://gamewith.net/zelda-ocarina-of-time/76561),
[GlitterBerri interview](https://glitterberri.com/ocarina-of-time/1101-interviews/minigames/),
[RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/feature/top-10-fishing-minigames-in-rpgs/))

**Twilight Princess.** Lures must be worked; the bobber lets you wait. Pulling back on a jump tends to break the
line. Its praise is mostly that the rod is a real item usable anywhere. Hena's pond: loaches in summer only, at the
lily pads, on the frog or sinking lure, hinted by a book in the hut (knowledge delivered in the world).
([GamesFirst](https://gamesfirst.com/articles/2006/twilight-princess-hands-on-with-wii/),
[Zelda Dungeon](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Hena%27s_Fishing_Hole))

**Sega Bass / Marine Fishing.** Lures work different depths; conditions matter (topwater in the morning, deeper on hot
days). Praised for "ravenous" fish and no hunting; Marine Fishing's big fish leap when hooked. The rod peripheral
added nothing over the pad: the feel is in the feedback, not the hardware.
([Gamebits](https://www.gamebits.net/segabass/), [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/2008/04/sega_bass_fishing_wii),
[GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/sega-marine-fishing-review/1900-2651278/))

**Final Fantasy XV.** The lure must suit the fish (weight sets depth). Hold the stick toward where the fish swims to
ease tension, don't reel while it's red, reel in bursts to drain stamina. Guides argue over watching the fish or the
gauge, a sign the gauge competes with the fish for attention.
([GamerGuides](https://gamerguides.com/final-fantasy-xv/guide/the-basics/skills/fishing),
[RPGSite](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/5109-final-fantasy-xv-fishing-guide-how-to-fish-properly-to-raise-your-fishing-level-and-earn-ap-quickly))

**Red Dead Redemption 2.** Lures must keep moving, bait can sit; flicking too often spooks fish. Tire the fish by
pulling against its movement, reel once it's tired; the line breaks if you reel while it fights. Players learned to
twitch, pause and watch the surface bulge as a fish closes in. Fast stick rotation to reel was tiring enough that
players switched to hold-to-reel.
([GTABase](https://gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide-how-to-fish-bait-and-fishing-tips),
[Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1174180/discussions/0/3184612071862622533))

**Sea of Thieves.** Pull against the fish's direction, reel when it tires; a wrong pull rumbles the controller and
creaks the rod. The clearest example of rumble and sound as the tension gauge.
([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/111513/how-to-go-fishing-in-sea-of-thieves))

**Bass Masters Classic, Legend of the River King, Reel Fishing.** Bass Masters: "each lake a little puzzle", no long
waits. River King: let it run, reel when it tires; bait and hook decide the species; 40 to catalogue; criticised as
cryptic, and the sequel for mashing. Reel Fishing's tension is "almost too easy" when fish have no personality.
([VideoGameCritic](https://videogamecritic.com/snesbb.htm), [Outcyders](https://www.outcyders.net/review/legend-of-river-king-review),
[Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/eshop/reel_fishing_3d_paradise_mini_eshop))

**Animal Crossing.** Nibble, nibble, splash-and-rumble bite, press A: the gold standard for a readable bite. Shadow
size tells the size class. Criticised for lottery rarity.
([Nintendo Wire](https://nintendowire.com/guides/animal-crossing-new-horizons/fishing/), [Pixel Poppers](https://pixelpoppers.com/2020/04/why-is-fishing-so-hard/))

**Warnings:** Stardew's bar has a response curve that fights the player, not the fish; Dredge's rings are "the same
thing ten minutes in or ten hours"; Fishing Resort's pose prompts are a QTE in disguise. Yakuza's fish silhouette
turning red when it looks at the hook is a nice, cheap "interested" tell.
([Stardew forums](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/fishing-is-impossible.2906/latest), [NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/dredge-review-3418763),
[GameRevolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/review/53956-fishing-resort-review), [PowerPyx](https://powerpyx.com/like-a-dragon-ishin-fishing-guide))

**Real angling the good games borrow.** The retrieve is the main variable: steady for cruising fish, twitch-and-pause
for fish near cover, stop-and-go (fall and rise like an injured baitfish). Fish often strike **on the pause**, and a
longer pause "turns followers into biters". Head shakes throw the hook; on a jump, keep the line tight with the rod
low and to the side. A reel's drag slips before the line breaks.
([FishUSA](https://www.fishusa.com/learn/bass-fishing-retrieval-methods/), [Wired2Fish](https://www.wired2fish.com/fishing-tips/how-to-fish-a-twitch-bait),
[Field & Stream](https://www.fieldandstream.com/articles/fishing/bass-fishing/2010/04/how-keep-jumping-largemouth-bass-line/),
[Bassmaster](https://www.bassmaster.com/tips/dave-wolak-setting-drag))

Design framing: approach (calm) → bite (a timed surprise) → catch (challenge); visibility from the real fish to a
shadow to an abstract meter, the further toward the meter the less satisfying.
([Game Developer, "Taxonomy of Fishing Mini-games"](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/taxonomy-of-fishing-mini-games))

## On the N64 pad

Keep OoT's own pond grammar, which players already know:

| Input | Lure, before the bite | Bobber, before the bite | Fight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rod button (B / C) | cast, hold for distance; tap = twitch | cast; tap = jiggle | |
| A | hold = steady retrieve, **release = pause** | reel in | hold = reel; **A + stick down = set the hook** |
| R | A+R = fast reel | | A+R = reel hard (risky) |
| Stick | sweep = jerk (size of the sweep = twitch vs jerk) | small nudge | **steer against the run**; ease off on a jump |
| Z | keep the camera on a fish that's following | on the bobber | on the fish |
| Rumble Pak | ticks on bumps | ticks per nibble, a strong pulse on the bite | scaled to tension, a warning pulse near breaking, bursts on head shakes |

Stop-and-go falls out of A naturally (release is the pause). No stick spinning to reel: it's tiring and wears out
N64 sticks. The rod bend and a line creak are the main tension gauge; any meter stays small.

## Ranked: adopt

1. **Fish as the readout:** visible states idle → notice → follow → inspect → strike, with a clear "interested" tell.
2. **Retrieve style as the lure skill:** steady / twitch / jerk / stop-and-go; each species has a preference; many
   strike on the pause.
3. **Fight by direction and stamina:** steer against the run, reel in the tired windows; the line breaks only from
   reeling into a run or holding against it too long.
4. **Three-cue bite rhythm** (bobber, sound, rumble), hook-set window opening instantly, its width by species.
5. **Rumble and rod bend as the tension gauge,** a creak near breaking; meter minimal.
6. **Telegraphed jumps and head shakes:** ease off on the jump, steady tension through shakes.
7. **OoT's input grammar** (above).
8. **Knowledge-gated rarity:** time, rain / Song of Storms, spot, bait; hinted in the world and the journal.
9. **Lures by depth and action** (topwater, diving, sinking, frog), matched to species and conditions.
10. **Failure that names its cause.**
11. **Spooking:** over-twitching, noise or a cast right on top of a fish scatters it.
12. **Journal:** counts, records, habit notes that unlock as you catch; a reward for completing it.
13. *(Optional)* **Slipping drag:** under a hard run the line pays out with a click before it snaps (our own
    extrapolation from real reels).

## Ranked: avoid

1. Abstract bars or boxes cut off from the fish (Stardew).
2. Timing rings and pose prompts (Dredge, Fishing Resort).
3. Mashing to reel (River King 2).
4. Spinning the stick to reel (RDR2; bad for N64 sticks).
5. Long waits with nothing to read; pure-chance rare fish (Animal Crossing).
6. Fishing as mandatory progression.
7. Line wear as a consumable to manage (FFXV): upkeep with no skill in it (our judgement).
8. Fish without personality in the fight (Reel Fishing).

**Not verified:** TP's exact GameCube fight controls, how Sega Bass Fishing's AI works inside, and the OoT sinking
lure unlock (sources conflict).
