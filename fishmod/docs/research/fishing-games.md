# Research notes

What the mod borrows from Twilight Princess and Sega Bass Fishing, and where it came from.

## Twilight Princess fishing

- **Bobber fishing anywhere.** In TP the Fishing Rod works in any area with water: a simple hook and a
  bobber on a line. Fish caught this way go in the Fish Journal. You can also reel in junk, like an empty can.
- **The bite.** A real bite pulls the bobber at least halfway under. Set the hook when the bobber is at its
  deepest point: too early and you miss, too late and the fish steals the bait.
- **Bait.** Bait only works on the bobber rod and makes bites more likely. Bee larvae come from knocking down
  beehives (Ordon Village, behind Hena's hut) and scooping the remains with an empty bottle, ten larvae per
  scoop.
- **Fish Journal.** Records each species with the number caught and the largest size.
- **Species.** Six journal fish: Hyrule Bass, Hylian Pike (the "star of jumping fish"), Greengill (found
  almost everywhere), Ordon Catfish, Hylian Loach (juveniles in the overworld, adults rare) and Reekfish (only
  near the Zoras' home, only on the Coral Earring).
- **Sizes seen in guides.** Greengill up to about 16 in, Hyrule Bass about 21 in on the bobber, young loaches
  about 12–15 in, Fishing Hole loaches up to about 30 in. Guides disagree on the exact numbers.

## Sega Bass Fishing

- **Fish show interest.** A bass that notices the lure follows it, and the camera often pans to show it.
  Rod flicks can coax a following fish into striking.
- **The hookset.** You have to set the hook right after the hit, or the fish swims off ("MISS").
- **Line tension.** During the fight, a tension gauge has to stay in the middle: too high and the line
  breaks, too low and the hook comes loose. Each lure behaves differently, and fish react to retrieve speed
  and rod movement.

## How the mod uses it

- TP's bobber loop is the base: cast, watch the bobber, read nibbles versus the real bite, set the hook at
  the deepest point.
- Fish are visible in the water and react to the bobber like Sega Bass Fishing bass: notice, approach,
  inspect, nibble, strike. Twitching the bobber can coax a hesitant fish.
- Fights use a Sega-style tension meter on top of TP's reel-and-pull.
- Each species has its own curiosity, aggression, wariness, strength, stamina, speed, jumpiness, nibble
  count and bite window (see the species pages).
