# Hardware bench 5i (F3DEX3)

## Findings
- **All 22 stops at 20 fps** (East weed flats 19.5 and 19.8); 5h had 13. Mean RDP 46.9 -> 32.0 ms.
- **F3DEX3 is not what did it.** With no props (the same scene on both benches) the RDP time is 23.2 -> 22.7 ms
  (-2%); at stops drawing about as many props as on 5h, 0-10% less. The win is fewer props: 5h drew 150-670 at the
  slow stops, 5i 40-200 (bigger, fewer moss mats; the re-thinning).
- **The per-prop setup is small:** colour and matrices with no triangles (mtxonly) cost 1.6 ms of the props' 9.2 ms
  (~0.015 ms a prop); the sway matrix costs nothing measurable. The fixed cost per prop in the model fitted on 5h was
  really its triangles. Merging props into shared meshes would save 1-2 ms: not worth it.
- **Moss:** 0.8 ms on average, but ~10 ms at the East weed flats.
- **Headroom:** most stops 10-20 ms under the 50 ms frame; tightest the East weed flats (RDP 44) and the beach
  shallows (43.8, a few large near plants).
- **Worst frames** (63-67 ms) at the East weed flats happen with no props too: CPU update there is 24-28 ms
  (the fish), not drawing.

# Hardware bench (original-hardware build)

Modes: **base**: as shipped; **nosway**: no sway matrix (one matrix per prop, not two); **mtxonly**: each prop's colour and matrices sent, not its triangles (the per-prop setup alone); **nomoss**: no moss mats; **nonear**: only the props past 500 units; **nofar**: only the props within 500 units; **noprops**: props not drawn (what the props cost); **nosky**: no props, no skybox.

RDP columns are shares of its cycles: **buf** = command buffer busy (fetching from RDRAM, or held up), **pipe** = drawing, **tmem** = loading textures.
