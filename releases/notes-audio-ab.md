# audio-ab

**`fish-oot-audio-ab.bps`**: the audio lead's A/B, built from `claude/sleepy-faraday-ve9g2u` at b7d93c1 as it is
(`fishmod/build.sh --script audio_ab --hw --texpack --colpack --meshpack --gcc-all`). Six laps on one power-on, every
lap clear dawn: the audio whole, without its RSP tasks, without the driver's update either, and round again. Each
lap's end sends the audio thread's own time by part (debug.log: FISHMOD-AUDIO). About 24 minutes. Checked in ares:
boots and draws (frame 600); the laps' switching not watched there.
