# qol5

qol4 (see [its notes](history/notes-qol4.md)) plus:
- **Clearing buttons:** in the pause menu, pressing a C button on the item (or equipment) already on it takes it off
  that button. The D-pad already worked this way.
- **The D-pad hides** again until something is on it (then empty directions show their arrows).
- **The cheaper fish update** (see [fishopt1's notes](history/notes-fishopt1.md)); fish behave the same.

Patches: `fish-oot-qol5-testmode.bps` (emulators), `fish-oot-qol5-hw-testmode.bps` (console).
