# Console test suite, 19 November (morning-results: runs/20261119-0829)

Eight bench runs on the console (four builds, twice each, A B C D A B C D); the replay didn't upload.

**Did they work.** All eight started and finished. Every build made the same sets of props on the console as in
ares (the same count and hash), and each build's two runs agree. The end was noticed 8 frames sooner than in ares
(3,757 against 3,765) in every run.

**What it says**

- The bare-metal GCC 14 is slower on the console at either level: 6-7% more CPU on the game thread, far outside
  the spread. Not adopted. (ares's exact profile had said 1.6% faster for -O2, against an older base and counting
  every thread; its frame log had said 3% slower. The console agrees with the frame log.)
- Every C file on GCC 10 costs nothing (inside the spread).
- The frame's period barely moves between builds (0.7% at most): this stretch is the RDP's, about 42 ms of every
  frame, not the CPU's.
- ares had this stretch locked at 50.14 ms with 2% of frames long and no hitches, and the RDP at 23 ms. On the
  console it's 55.4 ms, 40% long, 7% of pictures held long enough to see.
