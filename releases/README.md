# Test patches

| Patch | Contents |
|---|---|
| `fish-oot-v0.1.1.bps` | No terrain clipping for the line and fish, reeling fish in through the shallows, back to full speed ([notes](notes-v0.1.1.md)) |
| `fish-oot-kokiri-test1.bps` | First playtest: fishing in Kokiri Forest (6 species), charge casting, nibbles and bites, tension fights, keep-in-bottle or release, catch records |

Apply to an **Ocarina of Time NTSC 1.2 (US)** `.z64` ROM (MD5 `57a9719ad547c516342e1a15d5c28c3d`) with
[Flips](https://github.com/Alcaro/Flips) or the [online patcher](https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/).
See the main [README](../README.md#controls) for controls.

In Kokiri Forest, press **L** to put the rod on B (press it again to get your sword back). Empty bottles get filled
with fish you keep.
